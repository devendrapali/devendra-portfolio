How I work

A simple, four-step process

Instead of jumping into long, expensive builds, I start with a prototype — then refine it into a site that’s ready to launch.

  1. 01

    Planning & design

    Gathering requirements, understanding your goals and shaping the layout in Figma.

  2. 02

    Front-end development

    Building the structure in semantic HTML, styling with CSS and adding interaction with JavaScript.

  3. 03

    Testing & optimisation

    Checking every page across browsers and devices, then tuning speed and accessibility.

  4. 04

    Deployment & care

    Launching on your hosting, handing over an easy editor and staying on for maintenance.
